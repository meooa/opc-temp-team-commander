# 临时团队角色画像库

Use this reference when the user asks who to hire, where to find them, how to screen them, or how to combine AI and human collaborators.

## 1. AI Temporary Workers

AI roles should produce drafts, checks, and structured material. Keep final responsibility with the founder or a qualified human.

| AI Role | Inputs | Outputs | Human Review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 资料员 | topic, target user, source constraints | source list, outline, comparison table | factual accuracy, source quality |
| 文案初稿员 | audience, tone, product facts | post, email, proposal copy, FAQ | promise, tone, compliance |
| 质检员 | draft, checklist, acceptance criteria | issue list, risk list, defect table | final acceptance |
| 表格整理员 | raw table, column rules | cleaned table, duplicate list, confidence marks | sample rows and sensitive data |
| 任务秘书 | meeting notes, project goal | work order, milestone reminders, handoff list | priority and responsibility |
| 代码助理 | small automation goal, sample data | script draft, test cases, README draft | security, test, deployment |
| 客服脚本员 | customer scenarios, policy | reply templates, escalation rules | sensitive cases and final wording |

Default AI boundary:

```text
AI 只负责生成草稿、结构化材料和检查清单，不替代创始人的最终判断，也不直接接触未脱敏的个人信息、客户资料、学生信息、合同、付款记录或敏感业务数据。
```

## 2. Creative And Content Freelancers

Typical roles: PPT designer, poster designer, copywriter, editor, video cutter, photographer, courseware assistant.

Best channels:

- Warm network and previous collaborators for urgent client-facing work.
- Portfolio platforms and social/content platforms for style-sensitive work.
- Small studios when stable delivery, invoice, or backup capacity matters.

Screen for:

- comparable cases, source-file handoff habit, revision boundary, copyright awareness, and ability to ask clarifying questions.

Work-order focus:

- final format, source file, reference style, must-have content, forbidden style, revision rounds, third-party materials, case-display permission.

Red flags:

- "什么都能做" without questions.
- refuses source-file or material-rights discussion.
- uses templates, fonts, photos, music, or plugins without explaining rights.
- accepts unlimited vague revisions.

Respect notes:

- Do not use "再高级一点" as the only feedback. Tie feedback to concrete acceptance criteria.
- If the founder changes direction after approving the style, treat it as new scope.

## 3. Research And Data Collaborators

Typical roles: lead-list researcher, data cleaner, desk researcher, industry material organizer.

Best channels:

- referrals for trust-sensitive data.
- freelance/service marketplaces for bounded data tasks.
- AI first for template and classification rules, human second for verification.

Screen for:

- sample rows with source links, confidence marks, duplicate handling, and willingness to say "not found."

Work-order focus:

- data columns, allowed sources, forbidden sources, sample-check size, error tolerance, privacy boundary, delivery format.

Red flags:

- invented contacts or source-free rows.
- asks for excessive personal data.
- uses scraping or platform behavior that may violate rules without warning.

Respect notes:

- Pay for verification labor, not just row count.
- Do not punish the collaborator for honestly marking unavailable information.

## 4. Development And Automation Collaborators

Typical roles: small-tool developer, automation script helper, landing-page implementer, integration assistant.

Best channels:

- known developers for anything involving credentials or business systems.
- service provider/studio for production systems or ongoing maintenance.
- AI for prototype code, tests, documentation, and bug list.

Screen for:

- previous code or demo, ability to explain assumptions, test habit, handoff clarity, and security awareness.

Work-order focus:

- expected behavior, input/output examples, environment, credentials boundary, source handoff, test cases, maintenance scope, deployment owner.

Red flags:

- asks for full account access before scope is clear.
- no test or handoff plan.
- refuses to separate prototype from production.

Respect notes:

- "帮我顺手加一下" often becomes new scope. Confirm fee and time before adding features.
- Keep emergency availability explicit and paid if required.

## 5. Professional Service Providers

Typical roles: finance/tax firm, legal service, HR service, filming crew, development studio, event supplier.

Best channels:

- company websites, industry associations, referrals, previous vendors, local service ecosystems.

Screen for:

- contract subject, business license when relevant, invoice ability, case evidence, named delivery contact, after-sales boundary.

Work-order focus:

- statement of work, contract, invoice, liability, acceptance, after-sales, confidentiality, and dispute path.

Red flags:

- only oral promises for high-value work.
- refuses contract/invoice discussion when the task clearly needs it.
- unclear who actually delivers.

Respect notes:

- Providers are not employees. Manage the statement of work, not their internal process.

## 6. Consultants And Experts

Typical roles: legal/tax/HR consultation, industry expert, product adviser, architecture reviewer, policy interpreter.

Best channels:

- professional referrals, known experts, reputable organizations, public work with traceable identity.

Screen for:

- relevant experience, clear limits, evidence-based judgment, ability to produce a written memo or decision checklist.

Work-order focus:

- question list, context, decision needed, output format, call length, written notes, confidentiality.

Red flags:

- gives certainty without facts.
- replaces professional qualification with hype.
- refuses to state assumptions and limits.

Respect notes:

- Pay for judgment, not only pages or minutes.
- Do not ask experts to guarantee uncertain business outcomes.

## 7. Beijing Offline Support

Typical roles: event sign-in, material distribution, on-site guide, local collection, installation assistant, photography assistant, venue runner.

Best channels:

- trusted local groups, venue/community networks, previous collaborators, service providers for higher-risk tasks.

Screen for:

- location fit, punctuality, ID/real-name confirmation when appropriate, relevant experience, emergency communication.

Work-order focus:

- exact place, start/end time, access route, on-site contact, meal/transport, dress/tool requirement, safety boundary, backup plan, payment time.

Red flags:

- no reliable contact method.
- unclear whether they can reach the site on time.
- task involves minors, home visits, school spaces, customer devices, or sensitive files without permission and scope.

Respect notes:

- Pay for organizer-caused waiting, overtime, return trips, or location changes.
- Do not ask local helpers to break venue rules, traffic rules, platform rules, or safety rules.

## 8. Platform Workers And Local Service People

Typical roles: courier, delivery rider, driver, repair worker, installer, cleaning or moving service, on-demand local task worker.

Best channels:

- official platform/service channels, verified providers, known technicians for private-space tasks.

Screen for:

- platform verification, service scope, receipt/order record, customer permission, after-sales path.

Work-order focus:

- task boundary, address, materials, waiting time, safety, data/device access, customer confirmation, payment/extra fee rule.

Red flags:

- asks to bypass platform safety/payment rules for risky tasks.
- wants device/customer data access unrelated to the service.
- no clear identity for home, school, or office visits.

Respect notes:

- Do not transfer urgency into unsafe speed or illegal shortcuts.
- Pay for added waiting, distance, materials, parking, tolls, or changed scope when appropriate.
- Speak politely and keep complaints tied to concrete service issues.
