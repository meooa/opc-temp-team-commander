# Example Outputs

Use these examples to keep responses concrete and humane.

## Example 1: PPT Delivery For A One-Person Company

User: "下周五要给客户提案，我一个人做不完，帮我找临时团队。"

Triage:

- Founder-owned: client promise, final story, claims, budget, final acceptance.
- AI-first: client background整理, competitor table, first outline, acceptance checklist.
- Human delivery: PPT designer for visual system and 20-slide polish.
- Optional consultant: 30-minute business logic review if the topic is high-value or unfamiliar.
- Cut: animation, extra poster, video version, and any "顺便做个官网" request unless separately scoped.

Recommended team:

- Founder: client promise, final story, budget, final acceptance.
- AI temporary worker: outline, competitor table, draft slide copy, review checklist.
- Freelance PPT designer: visual system and 20-slide polish.
- Consultant if needed: 30-minute business logic review.

Work packages:

1. AI research pack: competitor summary, client pain points, first outline.
2. Founder decision pack: confirm story spine and key claims.
3. PPT design package: title page, visual style, 20-slide polish, export PDF.
4. Acceptance: claim accuracy, slide consistency, no invented data, source notes.

Respect note: do not ask the designer for unlimited "再高级一点" revisions. Define 2 revision rounds and tie feedback to the acceptance checklist.

Closeout:

- Save final PPT, PDF, source assets, font/material notes, payment record, and whether this designer is suitable for future proposal work.

## Example 2: Beijing Offline Event Support

User: "周末北京线下活动缺人，要临时请人。"

Recommended team:

- Founder: event promise, money, venue communication, emergency decision.
- Offline temporary support: sign-in, guidance, material distribution.
- Service provider: photography or livestream if quality matters.
- AI temporary worker: attendee FAQ, check-in script, contingency checklist.

Must clarify:

- Venue address, access rules, work hours, dress code, meal/transport, emergency contact, payment time, and safety boundaries.
- Whether the work touches minors, medical/education data, customer contact details, or payment records.

Respect note: pay for extended waiting or overtime caused by the organizer's changes.

Boundary clause:

```text
本任务仅包含签到、引导和物料发放。若现场临时增加搬运、拍摄、销售、延时等待或更换地点，应先确认额外费用、结束时间和安全边界。
```

## Example 3: Computer Repair / Field Service

User: "我以前做过啄木鸟电脑维修，现在想临时找维修师傅帮客户上门。"

Recommended mode:

- Prefer verified service provider or known technician for home/office visits.
- Do not send an unverified person into private spaces.
- Define scope: diagnosis, replacement, installation, data handling, after-sales, and invoice/receipt.
- Require customer permission before accessing personal files or devices.

Acceptance:

- Fault description, before/after photos if appropriate, replaced parts list, customer confirmation, data privacy confirmation, payment record.

Humanity:

- Do not hide customer risk or force unsafe time windows.
- Pay for diagnostic visit if the problem is not repairable but the technician has spent time and transport.

Data boundary:

```text
维修人员只处理本次故障相关设备和软件，不查看、复制或外传客户私人文件。若必须接触个人文件或账号，应先获得客户明确同意，并由客户在场操作。
```

## Example 4: AI + Human Operations Assistant

User: "我要做 200 个潜在客户名单和首封邮件。"

Recommended split:

- AI: generate industry segmentation, email template variants, validation checklist.
- Human freelancer: verify company names, websites, contact channels, remove duplicates, mark confidence.
- Founder: decide target segments and approve final outreach.

Data rule:

- Use public business data where possible.
- Avoid scraping or processing personal phone/email without considering privacy and platform rules.

Acceptance:

- Spreadsheet columns defined, source link required, confidence level required, no invented contacts, sample-check 20 rows before full payment.

Payment:

- Pay after sample acceptance for the first 20 rows, then continue in batches. Do not pay only for quantity if accuracy and source links are the real value.

## Example 5: Founder + AI + Human Product Launch Sprint

User: "我要一个人上线一个小产品，月底前要有落地页、说明文档、演示视频和第一批冷启动内容。"

Triage:

- Founder-owned: product promise, pricing, target customer, final launch decision.
- AI temporary workers: landing-page copy draft, FAQ, release checklist, demo script, bug checklist.
- Freelancer: landing page implementation or UI polish if founder lacks前端能力.
- Video collaborator: demo video剪辑 if the product needs public展示.
- Consultant: optional product/marketing review before spending more money.
- Cut: full品牌系统, 复杂后台, 多平台内容矩阵, unless launch proof already exists.

Work packages:

1. AI launch kit: landing copy, FAQ, cold-start post drafts, demo script.
2. Product handoff kit: screenshots, feature list, pricing, terms caveats.
3. Landing page delivery: one responsive page, analytics placeholder, deployment handoff.
4. Demo video: 60-90 seconds, source file, subtitles, export format.
5. Acceptance: no fake claims, no private data in demo, links work, mobile readable.

Humanity:

- Do not compress three different professional jobs into one vague "运营搭子" role. Split design, implementation, and video if quality matters.

## Example 6: Platform Worker / Local Errand With Respect

User: "我临时需要人帮我在北京跑一趟，顺便取东西、等客户签字、再送到另一个地方。"

Triage:

- If it is simple delivery, use official courier/platform process and pay for distance/waiting.
- If it involves contract, payment record, ID material, student/customer data, or valuable goods, use verified provider or trusted person.
- If the task requires negotiation or legal responsibility, do not treat it as a simple errand.

Work order:

```text
任务：取文件并送达指定地点
地点：A 点取件，B 点送达
时间：预计开始/最晚送达
材料：密封文件袋，不拆封、不拍照、不外传
等待：超过约定等待时间后按每 30 分钟补偿
变更：更换地址、增加等待、增加取送点，先确认费用
验收：送达照片/签收确认/平台订单记录
```

Humanity:

- Do not要求对方违法停车、危险赶路、免费等待、私下绕开平台保障，或承担未说明的贵重物品风险。

## Example 7: Standard Final Section

```text
协作边界：
本任务采用成果交付方式。委托方只定义目标、交付物、截止时间、验收标准和合规边界；承接方可自主决定工作方法、工具、时间安排和内部协作方式。除约定交付节点外，不要求随时在线或接受额外未约定任务。

人文关怀检查：
- 任务范围已写清，避免用“感觉不对”代替标准。
- 试工任务不构成免费生产劳动。
- 沟通节点清楚，不要求 24 小时在线。
- 新增需求需要重新确认费用和时间。
- 个人信息和客户资料先脱敏再流转。
- 因委托方原因导致等待、返场、超时或更换地点，应先确认补偿。

结项复盘：
- 本次最可靠的协作者是谁。
- 哪个环节下次可交给 AI 先做。
- 哪个验收标准下次要提前写得更硬。
- 哪个协作者不适合再次合作，原因是什么。
```
