# 一人公司临时团队指挥官 Playbook

Use this reference when a request needs detailed work orders, recruitment messages, trial tasks, scoring, acceptance templates, payment/change rules, or closeout records.

## 0. Delivery Loop

Use this loop when the user wants a complete temporary-team plan.

| Stage | Founder Owns | AI Helps With | Human/Provider Owns | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Define | client promise, budget, final standard | clarify brief, draft checklist | asks clarifying questions | one-page task brief |
| 2. Split | priority and scope cuts | package draft, dependency map | confirms feasibility | work packages |
| 3. Recruit | channel choice, final selection | message drafts, scoring sheet | portfolio, quote, schedule | candidate shortlist |
| 4. Start | kickoff decision, payment node | kickoff notes, risk checklist | delivery plan | confirmed scope |
| 5. Accept | final judgment and payment | acceptance checklist, defect list | revisions against standard | accepted handoff |
| 6. Archive | talent ledger and lessons | summary, reusable templates | optional case permission | closeout record |

If the user has no time, compress stages 1-3 into a same-day plan but keep the acceptance and payment boundaries explicit.

## 1. Team Modes

### Founder + AI

Use when the task is low-risk, mostly textual, repeatable, or exploratory.

Typical tasks: research outline, first draft, checklist, spreadsheet cleanup, customer FAQ, competitor table, task decomposition, test case drafting.

Founder must keep: final judgment, sensitive data handling, client promise, and quality acceptance.

### AI First + Human Review

Use when speed matters but quality still needs human taste or domain judgment.

Typical tasks: PPT outline, poster copy, video script, operating SOP, landing page draft, data-tagging rule draft.

Human reviewer checks: factual accuracy, tone, local context, copyright risk, and whether the output actually serves the client.

### Freelancer Outcome Delivery

Use for bounded deliverables with clear files and acceptance standards.

Typical tasks: design, editing, PPT polishing, copywriting, automation scripts, one-off research, short video clips.

Avoid when the user wants fixed daily attendance, long-term exclusive control, or indefinite work without scope.

### Professional Service Provider / Studio

Use when work needs organizational responsibility, invoice, equipment, repeated service capacity, or local execution.

Typical tasks: event setup, shooting crew, legal/tax/accounting, product development project, ad operation, brand design package.

Check: business license, case evidence, invoice ability, contract terms, delivery team stability, and after-sales handling.

### Consultant / Expert

Use when the value is judgment rather than execution.

Typical tasks: policy interpretation, industry review, architecture review, education/medical/legal/finance risk judgment, pricing strategy, channel strategy.

Set output as: diagnostic memo, review call, written recommendations, or decision checklist.

### Offline Temporary Support

Use for short on-site work.

Typical tasks: event reception, field collection, exhibition support, inventory, simple customer guidance, local errands.

Set boundaries: exact location, time window, safety, emergency contact, meal/transport arrangement, replacement plan, and payment timing.

## 2. Channel Types In Beijing / China Mainland

Do not promise a specific platform is currently best unless you verify it. Recommend channel categories and selection logic.

- Warm network: friends, industry WeChat groups, alumni groups, customer referrals. Best for trust-sensitive work.
- Local community channels: Beijing neighborhood groups, maker spaces, co-working communities, school communities, event groups. Best for offline temporary support.
- Vertical freelancer/service marketplaces: useful for design, editing, copywriting, development, and professional services. Verify cases and platform rules.
- Professional service providers: company websites, industry associations, local agencies, accounting/legal/tax/service firms. Best when invoice and responsibility matter.
- Content/social platforms: Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, WeChat public accounts, Zhihu, Douyin creators. Best for creators and niche experts; verify identity and past work.
- AI tools/agents: best for drafts, repetitive tasks, checks, data organization, and internal copilots; keep human review.
- Existing vendor/customer ecosystem: ask previous suppliers, partners, or clients for a known reliable person.

Channel choice rule: trust-sensitive first through known people; quality-sensitive through portfolio; compliance-sensitive through providers; urgent offline tasks through local groups with backup.

## 5. Trial Task Design

Use a trial task only to reduce risk, not to obtain free work.

Good trial task:

- Small: 30-90 minutes when unpaid and non-usable, or paid if longer/usable.
- Representative: tests the real skill, not trivia.
- Clear: same input, same acceptance criteria for all candidates.
- Respectful: no hidden production work.
- Reversible: does not require access to sensitive client, student, payment, or identity data.

Scoring rubric:

| Dimension | Score | What To Check |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Understanding | 1-5 | Did they restate the task correctly and ask useful questions? |
| Quality | 1-5 | Is the output usable against the stated standard? |
| Reliability | 1-5 | Did they meet the promised time and format? |
| Communication | 1-5 | Clear, calm, boundary-aware, no overpromise? |
| Risk Awareness | 1-5 | Did they notice copyright, data, safety, or feasibility issues? |

Red flags:

- Promises everything without asking inputs.
- Refuses clear acceptance standards.
- Pushes for full payment before any trust exists without credible proof.
- Uses obvious copied samples without source clarity.
- Requires access to excessive sensitive data.
- Accepts unlimited changes or impossible deadlines just to win the job.
- Treats worker safety, privacy, or customer permission as optional.

## 6. Payment And Change Control

Use simple staged payment unless the task is tiny and low-risk.

Common patterns:

| Task Type | Suggested Payment | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small one-off task | full payment after acceptance or small deposit + final | keep acceptance standard simple |
| Creative/design/writing | deposit + first draft acceptance + final | define revision rounds |
| Development/automation | milestone by function or handoff | include test and source handoff |
| Offline local support | same-day or next-day settlement | define overtime/waiting/transport |
| Professional provider | contract-based milestone | confirm invoice and legal subject |
| Consultant review | pay by memo/call/session | require written conclusion when useful |

Change confirmation wording:

```text
这个需求不在原任务范围内。可以做，但需要先确认三件事：新增交付物、增加费用、调整后的交付时间。确认后我再安排继续。
```

Revision vs new scope:

- Revision: fixing defects against the original acceptance criteria.
- New scope: new audience, new format, new content, new platform, new data, new style direction after approval, extra meeting, extra delivery channel, or additional revision round.

## 7. Milestone Operating Rhythm

Use this rhythm for real projects:

```text
T0 启动：确认目标、资料、交付物、验收标准、付款节点。
T1 小样：先看方向，不做大规模生产。
T2 首版：按验收清单检查，不用模糊感受压人。
T3 返工：只针对原标准内缺陷；新增需求另算。
T4 终版：交付源文件/记录/账号交接/删除证明。
T5 结项：付款、归档、记录是否可复用。
```

## 9. Human Respect Check

Before finalizing a plan, check:

- Is the scope clear enough that the collaborator will not be punished for guessing wrong?
- Is the deadline compatible with the workload?
- Is the payment rule explicit and not intentionally vague?
- Is trial work proportionate and not free production labor?
- Are communication expectations humane?
- Is process autonomy respected?
- Are changes priced or renegotiated?
- Are privacy, safety, and dignity protected?
- For platform/local workers, are waiting, distance, materials, weather, and safety costs recognized?
- For experts, is the request framed as judgment rather than asking them to guarantee uncertain outcomes?
